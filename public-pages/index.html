What's inside

Everything your school office already does is connected.

Six areas cover the day-to-day work of running a school, and every role only sees what they need.

Admissions & Records

Admission numbers, guardian details, class assignment and full academic history in one profile.

Attendance

Class-based or subject-based, marked in under a minute, with instant absence reports to parents.

Grading & Reports

Configure your own weighting including class tests, quizzes, exams and allow the system to automatically total and grade.

Fees & Payments

Invoices, MoMo and card collection, payroll and scholarships, reconciled automatically.

Parent Portal

Attendance, fees, announcements and a report card the moment it's cleared - not a moment before.

Security & Gate Log

Who dropped a student off, who picked them up, logged in real time and visible to parents.
