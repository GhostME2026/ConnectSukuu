Trust, privacy and student protection

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Important: ConnectSukuu is a school-management platform. A school using the service decides which student, parent and staff information it enters and why. Depending on the service arrangement, the school will generally determine the purposes of processing while ConnectSukuu processes information to provide and secure the platform. This policy should be reviewed against each school’s actual contract and configuration.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to ConnectSukuu’s public website and school-management services, including school administrators, owners, teachers, students, parents and guardians, nurses, accountants and security staff who use an authorised account.

2. Information we may process

  • Account and identity information: names, staff roles, account identifiers, email addresses, telephone numbers, profile photographs and authentication records.
  • School and learning information: school and branch details, admissions numbers, class and subject enrolments, attendance, assessments, report cards, learning materials and academic progress.
  • Parent and guardian information: contact details, authorised relationships to a student, and relevant communication preferences.
  • Operational information: school-entry and exit logs, messages, announcements, support requests, fee records, payment references and audit/security logs.
  • Technical information: device/browser details, IP and access logs, session events, and diagnostic information needed to operate and protect the service.
  • Health information: where a school uses health or nurse features, records the school is authorised to maintain. These records require heightened access controls and should only be entered for a legitimate school purpose.

3. Why information is used

Information may be used to provide school administration features; manage enrolment, teaching, attendance, assessments and reporting; enable parent/guardian access; deliver messages and notifications; administer fees and subscriptions; record authorised school-gate activity; respond to support requests; prevent fraud and unauthorised access; maintain backups and audit trails; and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Legal grounds and school responsibilities

Processing must have an appropriate lawful basis for the particular purpose, such as performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, a properly assessed legitimate interest, or consent where consent is the appropriate basis. Schools must determine and document the lawful basis for their use of student and family information, provide any required notices, and obtain parental/guardian authorisation where required. Consent is not assumed merely because a school has created an account.

5. Ghanaian data-protection framework

ConnectSukuu is intended to be operated consistently with applicable Ghanaian law, including the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). Depending on the activity, other relevant legislation may include the Cybersecurity Act, 2020 (Act 1038), the Electronic Transactions Act, 2008 (Act 772), the Children’s Act, 1998 (Act 560), and applicable education and records requirements. References are general and do not replace legal advice. The school and ConnectSukuu must each assess their own legal duties, including any applicable registration or notification requirements with the Data Protection Commission.

6. Children and student records

Student information must be handled for legitimate educational, safeguarding, administrative or legally required purposes. Access should be limited to authorised users according to their role. Schools should verify parent/guardian authority, avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information, and take particular care before sharing, exporting or publishing student records. Public disclosure of a student’s information or photograph should not occur without an appropriate lawful basis and any required permissions.

7. Sharing and service providers

Information may be shared with authorised users within the relevant school and branch, and with service providers needed to host, secure, maintain or deliver the service, including database hosting, file storage, payment processing, email/SMS delivery and technical support. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the rights, safety and security of users. We do not sell student or school personal information to advertisers.

Schools should ensure that recipients, parent/guardian links, SMS destinations and exported files are accurate and authorised. SMS and third-party services may apply their own privacy terms and delivery practices.

8. International processing

Depending on the hosting and service providers selected, information may be processed or stored outside Ghana. Where this occurs, the parties responsible for the processing should assess applicable transfer requirements and use appropriate contractual, technical and organisational safeguards.

9. Security

ConnectSukuu uses role-based access, account authentication, access controls and operational safeguards intended to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users must protect their credentials, sign out of shared devices, report suspected compromise promptly and only access information required for their duties. Schools should promptly remove access when a staff member no longer needs it.

10. Retention and deletion

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet school and legal recordkeeping requirements, resolve disputes, maintain security and enforce agreements. Retention periods may differ by record type and by the school’s instructions. When information is no longer required, it should be securely deleted or de-identified where appropriate, subject to backups and lawful retention duties.

11. Your rights and requests

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights to request access to personal information, correction of inaccurate information, objection to or restriction of certain processing, withdrawal of consent where consent is the basis, or other available remedies. Student or parent/guardian requests should ordinarily be directed to the relevant school first. ConnectSukuu will support the school’s response where it acts as a service provider. A request may require identity and authority verification.

12. Cookies, logs and analytics

The website or application may use essential browser storage and session technologies to maintain sign-in, preferences, security and service operation. Server logs may be used to diagnose errors, protect the platform and investigate misuse. If non-essential analytics or advertising technologies are introduced, the relevant notice and consent choices will be updated as required.

13. Incident response and changes

We will assess suspected security incidents and take reasonable steps to contain and investigate them, notify affected schools or individuals where required, and cooperate with competent authorities as applicable. We may update this policy when the service or law changes. The effective date above indicates the latest revision date.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, access requests or concerns, contact ConnectSukuu at connectsukuu@gmail.com or +233 20 891 6616. The service is based in Ghana. A school may also need to contact its own designated privacy contact or the Ghana Data Protection Commission.
