Why we built this

Schools don't run on software built for offices.

Most school platforms are generic record-keeping systems with a fresh coat of paint. ConnectSukuu starts from the register, the assessment sheet, the gate log your school already keeps and turns them into a system every role can use from a phone.

How it works

From admission to transcript, one record.

1

Owner delegates the roles

The school owner signs up, adds branches if there are any, and assigns a head teacher, administrator, head of academics, accountant and the rest.

2

Administrator builds the record

Admission numbers, guardian details, class and subject assignment — set up once, read by every other role.

3

Teachers teach and assess

Attendance, homework and quizzes, and a grading sheet that totals everything against your school's own weighting.

4

Reports reach parents, cleared

Academic head reviews, head teacher endorses, and a link goes out by SMS or email — once, and correctly.

Our approach

Built for how Ghanaian schools actually operate.

Mobile Money for fees. SMS where data isn't guaranteed. A grading structure that matches the SBA system your teachers already use — not one borrowed from a system built for somewhere else.

Payments that fit

Mobile Money and card, side by side, because that's how Ghanaian parents actually pay school fees.

SBA-style grading

Configurable assessment weighting that matches the continuous assessment system your teachers already know.

SMS-first communication

Report links and alerts sent by SMS as well as email, because data isn't guaranteed for every parent.
