Multi-campus administration

Every campus has its own leadership.

Create a branch first, then appoint its Administrator and Head Teacher from Team & Roles. Branch deactivation preserves existing student, staff and fee history.

Workflow:  Create the branch → assign one Administrator and one Head Teacher → assign all other staff to that campus. Accounts without an active branch assignment cannot use branch-restricted services.
